- `public/art/abyss.webp`: generated NEREID submersible artwork, reused from `IseFDK/abyss-expedition` (`public/images/abyss-submersible-hero.webp`). Original creation prompt/provenance lives in that project's assets. Fictional vehicle; not a photograph or engineering-certified design.
- `public/art/riso.webp`: generated digital risograph sheet, reused from `IseFDK/riso-riot` (`public/art/riso-sheet.webp`). Provenance: that repository's `public/art/riso-sheet-provenance.txt`. Digital visual, not a physical print guarantee.
- `public/art/aura.webp`: generated Greninja fan-art, reused from `IseFDK/aura-atlas` (`public/hero-greninja.webp`). Provenance: that repository's `artwork/hero-greninja-provenance.json`. Franchise rights remain with the relevant Pokémon rights holders.
- NOCTURNE route diagram: portfolio-native factual diagram of Vesper → Selene → Aether, verified against `https://github.com/IseFDK/nocturne-observatory/blob/main/README.md`. Not a screenshot, photograph or astronomical map.
- LEDGER architecture diagram: portfolio-native diagram based on `https://github.com/IseFDK/ledger-diploma/blob/main/README.md`. Not a screenshot or financial data.
- Fonts: self-hosted Manrope from the RISO RIOT project, SIL OFL 1.1; upstream authorship and license in `public/fonts/OFL.txt`.

All three raster images were visually inspected before integration. Optimization changes resolution/compression only. Original raster outputs are retained in their existing creation workspaces.
